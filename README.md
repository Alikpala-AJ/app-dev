# Avatar (2009)

> *"Everything is backwards now, like out there is the true world, and in here is the dream."* — **Jake Sully**

## Plot Summary

Set in the year 2154, Earth's natural resources have been severely depleted. The **Resources Development Administration (RDA)** establishes a mining colony on **Pandora**, a lush, bioluminescent habitable moon, to extract a rare mineral called *unobtanium*. Because Pandora's atmosphere is toxic to humans and the richest deposits lie beneath the ancestral home of the indigenous **Na'vi**, the RDA launches the **Avatar Program**.

The program allows human operators to remotely control genetically engineered Na'vi-human hybrid bodies through a neural link. **Jake Sully**, a paraplegic former Marine, is recruited to replace his deceased twin brother as an avatar operator.

Upon entering his avatar body, Jake regains the ability to walk and explores Pandora's wilderness. After getting separated from his team, he is rescued by **Neytiri**, a fierce Na'vi warrior of the Omatikaya clan. Over three months, Jake learns the ways of her people, bonds with the native wildlife, and falls in love with Neytiri. When RDA military leader **Colonel Miles Quaritch** prepares to destroy the Na'vi's sacred Hometree by force, Jake must choose between his human orders and uniting the clans of Pandora to defend their world.

---

### Main Characters

- **Jake Sully** – A former Marine who joins the Avatar Program and falls in love with Pandora.
- **Neytiri** – A Na'vi princess and warrior who teaches Jake the ways of her clan.
- **Colonel Miles Quaritch** – The ruthless head of the RDA's private security force.
- **Dr. Grace Augustine** – An exobiologist and head of the Avatar Program who advocates for peace with the Na'vi.
